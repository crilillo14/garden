Bertsekas is a pretty famous electrical engineer, kinda goated, seems to be really opinionated about the importance of control theory in finetuning and designing RL control loops for LLMs. 

More than a swift introduction to control theory, approximate dynamic programming (approximate due to intractability of exact DP), and bellman eqs and operators, this textbook puts _heavy_ emphasis that optimizations to the expected utility of policies are essentially ___Newton steps on Bellman Operators__.

Which is more of a fun fact, but I'm sure a seasoned control theorist would find this connection or approach to MPC and adaptive control exciting. This is in no way a survey or course text, more so a monograph of his research.

All in all, it seems to be a good intro. Coupling this with Algorithms for Decision Making Under Uncertainty, Algorithms for Optimization ([Kochenderfer](https://algorithmsbook.com/optimization/)) was very pleasant, especially  DM.pdf, part II: sequential games.
