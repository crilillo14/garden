Gibbs measures are discussed (& derived either independently or borrowed) in at least 5 major branches of mathematics, CS, and physics.

The most natural and straightforward derivation is found (imo) in information theory. 

_driving question_
Given a family of measures $\mathbf{P}$, where $p \in \mathbf{P}$ has some contstraint $G(p) = c \in \mathbb{R}$, G is an operator,  what is the _maximally entropic_ $p_{e} \in \mathbf{P}$ ? 

Answer: the gibbs measure, defined as the following measure P$p_{e} = \max_{p \in \mathbf{ P}} H(p) = \max_{p \in \mathbf{P}} \int_{\Omega}x\log p(x)dx$
